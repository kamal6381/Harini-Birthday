Nammoda friendship start aanathu BEEE sir moolama dhaan. Appo andha vishayam enakku sariya puriyala; aana ippo thirumbi yosikkumbodhu, andha chinna beginning namma friendship-ku evlo special-aana start-nu puriyuthu.

Apram crib decoration-ku serndhu work pannom. Konjam konjam-ah pesi, chinna chinna vishayangal share panni, normal friends-la irundhu best friends-a maarinom. Daily gossip, random talks, sirippu, namma problems and pain — ellathayum share panna aarambichom. Ippo andha moments ellam enakku romba precious.

Naan low-va irukkumbodhu en kooda irundhavanga neraya per illa. Aana nee irundha. Adhu enakku romba meaningful. Naan enna feel panren, enna nadakkuthunu, enna pathi un kitta open-ah share panna mudiyuthu. Idhuvaraikkum naan yaar kittayum sollaadha sila vishayangalai kooda un kitta sollirukken. Un kitta naan real-ah irukka mudiyuthu-nu feel panren. Maybe adhanaala dhaan namma friendship enakku innum emotional-ah special aayiduchu.

Un life-la nee neraya hurt aagirukka; neraya things face pannirukka. Aana andha pain un strength-ah maarattum. Unna easy-ah yaarayum namba vechidaadha; un personal vishayangalai yaar kittayum share panradhukku munnaadi, avanga un trust-ku worth-ah irukkaangala-nu paaru. Nee low-va irundhaalum, un feelings-ai hide pannanum-nu illa — aana un worth-ai marandhudaadha. Unna vittu pona people-kitta un happiness-ai tie panni vechikaadha. Inime un kooda irukkura, unakku nalladhu ninaikkura people-ai appreciate pannu; most importantly, unna neeye care pannikko.

Sometimes naan yosippen — naan unakku evlo important-nu enakku theriyala. Maybe naan just oru friend-a irukkalaam; naan romba pesuradhu unakku pudikkudha illaya-nu kooda enakku theriyadhu. Sila neram naan unakku thollaiya irukkeno-nu kooda thonum. Aana en side-la irundhu sollanum-na, nee enakku romba important. Athey maathiri, naan unakku evlo special-nu enakku theriyama irukkalaam. Aana nee enakku appovum, ippovum, eppovum special dhaan. ❤️

Naan happy-ah irundha first call panna thonra person nee. Sad-ah irundha, yosikkaama call panna thonra person-um nee dhaan. Adhu unakku naan evlo close-ah feel panren-nu sollum. Namma friendship eppadi change aanaalum, indha memories-um nee en life-la irundha place-um enakku special-ah dhaan irukkum. ❤️

Once again, Happy Birthday, eruma! 😂❤️ Past is past. Inime un life-ai unakkaga pudhusa start pannu. Unna neeye care pannu, un peace-ku importance kudu, unnecessary tension eduthukkaadha. Ellaar kitteyum same expectations vechikaama, un happiness-aiyum dreams-aiyum first place-la vai.

Indha year unakku happiness, peace, growth, good memories, and neraya reasons to smile kondu varattum. Nee deserve panra nalla vishayangal ellam un life-la nadakkanum. Be happy, be safe, and keep being you. ✨

Happy Birthday, Eruma! ❤️
